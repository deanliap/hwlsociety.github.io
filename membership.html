Membership

This is your
community.

HWLS membership is open to women 18+ with a connection to Hellenic culture or a passion for supporting Hellenic women's advancement across the Midwest.

As a member you'll gain access to professional events, a growing network of Hellenic women leaders, mentorship opportunities, and a community that celebrates both your ambition and your roots.

HWLS is pleased to offer free membership for the first year.

2026

Join before August 2026.
Be part of the origin story.

All members who enroll on or before August 31, 2026 will be permanently recognized as Founding Members of HWLS — an honor that stays with you for the lifetime of your membership.

  • Permanently listed as a Founding Member in HWLS records
  • Founding Member reduced dues in future years
  • Priority access to HWLS events and programming
  • Your name woven into the foundation of what HWLS becomes
